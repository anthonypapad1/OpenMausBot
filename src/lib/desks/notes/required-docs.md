The documents every deal file needs. Edit this list to match your brokerage: add, remove, or change what is required and when it is due.

Each line: document · required, optional, or required if built before 1978 · due (calendar days from the contract date, or "at closing", or "-" for no fixed date).

## Both sides

- Purchase & sale contract (fully executed CREC Contract to Buy and Sell) · required · day 0
- Counterproposals & amendments · optional · -
- Source of Water Addendum (§10.8) · required · day 0
- Earnest money receipt · required · day 3
- Title commitment · required · day 10
- HOA documents (if there is an association) · optional · day 14
- Closing instructions (§12.2) · required · at closing
- Closing disclosure / settlement statement · required · at closing

## Buyer side

- Brokerage disclosure to buyer (C.R.S. 12-10-408) · required · day 0
- Lead-based paint disclosure, signed by the buyer and every required licensee (§10.10.1) · required if built before 1978 · day 7
- Loan pre-approval letter or proof of funds · required · day 5
- Inspection report · required · day 10
- Inspection objection & resolution · optional · day 14
- Appraisal · required · day 21
- Loan commitment letter · required · day 25
- Homeowners insurance binder · required · day 27

## Seller side

- Brokerage disclosure to seller (C.R.S. 12-10-408) · required · day 0
- Seller's property disclosure · required · day 7
- Lead-based paint disclosure, signed by the seller and every required licensee (§10.10.1) · required if built before 1978 · day 7
- Square footage disclosure · optional · -
- Payoff statement · required · day 28
- Deed · required · day 29
