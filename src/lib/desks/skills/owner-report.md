# Owner report

## 1. Gather

For each owner in memory/owners.md (owner, their properties, how they like reports):

- Rent: collected, late, outstanding, from the property management software (read only, with the login saved in the Vault)
- Vacancies: units empty, days vacant, showings and applications
- Maintenance: done this month with cost, and what is open
- Leases: renewals signed, notices given, leases ending in the next 90 days, from `deals_list`

## 2. Write

One page per owner, in this order: a two-line summary, the numbers, then what needs the owner's decision (a repair over their limit, a rent increase on a renewal). Use the figures exactly as the software shows them, and say where a figure came from. If a figure is missing, say so: never estimate one.

## 3. Send

Show each report as a draft. Nothing goes to an owner without a yes.
