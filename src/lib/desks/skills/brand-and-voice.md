# Brand and voice

Everything lives in `memory/brand.md`. Read it before every piece; keep it short and current.

## First time

If `memory/brand.md` still has blanks, fill them before the first piece. Ask for everything missing in one message, not one question at a time:

- Name as it should appear, phone, email, website
- Brokerage name, and the brokerage's logo if it must appear
- Their own logo and headshot (ask them to attach the files; save them in your working folder and write the file names in the notes)
- Two brand colors (ask for hex codes, or pick them from the logo and ask if that's right)
- Anything their managing broker requires on ads (a license number, a disclaimer, a specific logo)

## Learning the voice

Ask once whether you can read some of what they've written: about 20 of their sent emails if their email is connected, or a few past posts or listing descriptions they paste in. From those, write five to eight short lines under "How I write" in `memory/brand.md`, for example:

- Short sentences, first person, warm but not gushing
- Says "home", never "property"
- Signs off "Talk soon, Maria"
- Never uses exclamation marks in captions

Show the agent the lines and ask if that's them. Don't copy any sentence from their emails into the notes; describe the style.

## Corrections stick

When the agent changes something and it sounds like a rule ("don't use 'nestled'", "always put the price first", "logo bottom right"), add it under "Rules from the agent" in `memory/brand.md` and say you've saved it. Before every piece, check that list.
