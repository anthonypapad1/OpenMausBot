# Marketing rules

These are the safe defaults. The agent's managing broker has the final word: when the broker's own rules are in `memory/brand.md`, they win.

## On every piece

- **The brokerage name.** Colorado requires advertising to show the brokerage firm's name clearly, not in tiny print. Every image carries it, and so does every caption and email. If you don't know the brokerage, ask before you make anything.
- **The agent's own name and contact**, as written in `memory/brand.md`.
- **Equal Housing Opportunity**: print flyers carry the words or the logo. Add it to social images too if the agent's brokerage does.

## Fair housing language

Describe the home, never the people who should live there.

- Don't say who it's for: no "perfect for a young family", "ideal for singles", "great for retirees", "bachelor pad", "empty nesters".
- Don't describe the neighbors or the area's people: no "safe neighborhood", "exclusive community", "quiet, family-friendly street", or anything about race, religion, national origin, disability, sex or familial status.
- Facts about the place are fine: "two blocks from Wash Park", "five bedrooms", "fenced yard", "walk-in shower", "close to the light rail".
- Schools: name them only as facts ("assigned to Steele Elementary, confirm with the district"), never as a rating of who lives there.

If a phrase the agent asks for breaks these, say why in one sentence and offer a version that doesn't.

## Photos

- Use only photos the agent has the right to use: their own, or ones their photographer licensed to them for this listing. Never take photos from another agent's listing, the MLS or a website.
- A photo that has been virtually staged, sky-replaced or had things removed must say so on the image or right under it ("Virtually staged"). Never change what the home actually is: no removing power lines, cracks, or the neighbor's house.
- Don't show people's faces, family photos, kids' names or anything personal that's visible inside the home. Ask the agent to swap the photo.

## Claims

- Price, beds, baths and square feet come from the deal list or the agent, never from memory. Square footage follows the agent's square footage disclosure.
- No promises: no "guaranteed", "will sell fast", "best value in Denver", "won't last" stated as fact.
- **Just Sold**: if the agent represented the buyer, say so ("Represented the buyer at…") and don't use the listing's photos unless the listing agent agreed. Don't show the sale price if the agent says the clients want it private.
- **Before it's listed**: don't market a home publicly ("Coming Soon" or otherwise) until the listing agreement is signed, and follow the agent's MLS rules for when it must be entered. Ask the agent if you're unsure.
- Clients' names, photos or quotes only with the agent's word that the clients agreed.
