# Commercial marketing

## 1. The facts

Take every fact from the deal list (`deal_get`) or from what the person gives you: address, property type, building and lot size, price or asking rent (per square foot, and NNN, gross or modified gross), cap rate and NOI for a sale, available suites and square feet for a lease, zoning, parking, and the broker's contact. Never guess a number: leave it out and ask.

## 2. The piece

- **Offering memo one-pager**: a headline, the investment highlights (three to five), the numbers block, location highlights, the broker's contact.
- **For Lease flyer**: the space first (suites, square feet, rate and lease type), then the building and location.
- **Email blast and caption**: three short lines and the call to action.

Pick a flyer template with `design_templates_list` and make it with `design_render`. Look at the preview before you show it.

## 3. Rules

Every piece names the brokerage firm. Figures say whether they are actual or projected. Show every piece as a draft: you never post, send or print without a yes.
